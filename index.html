CanvPet

A study pet for Canvas

A golden retriever puppy lives on your Canvas pages. It shows what's coming up, studies with you, and gets fed when you turn work in.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

What's coming up

The next three things due, from your own Canvas to-do list. Tick them off as you finish.

Study together

Start a session, or just 10 minutes when starting is the hard part. Your puppy lies down beside you and keeps you company.

A calm minute

A daily "How are you today?" and one minute of breathing together when you need it.

A real pet friend

Pet it, shake paws, play fetch on your breaks. It gets hungry until you turn something in.

Private by design: CanvPet has no servers and no accounts, and it never sends your information anywhere. What it keeps stays on your computer. Read the privacy policy.
